THE QUESTIONS THAT MATTER

Let's get
the details clear.

Do I need to know what AI tool I want?

No. Start with the job and the result you need. Choosing a useful approach belongs in scoping.

Can this work with our existing software?

That's where we start. We check what can connect, what access is needed and whether the existing tools can support the project.

How are the budget and timeline decided?

They're agreed for the specific project after the workflow and builder fit have been checked. You should see the scope, price and milestones before committing.

Who actually builds the system?

An independent builder whose relevant work and approach have been reviewed for your project. XASIS handles the brief, matching and coordination.

What if AI isn't the right answer?

We say so. Sometimes a simpler automation, a better use of existing software or a change to the process is a better fit.

What happens if the scope changes?

A new requirement should be discussed before it turns into extra work. Agree the effect on scope, price and timing together.
