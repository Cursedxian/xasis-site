BEFORE WE WORK TOGETHER

A few things
to get straight.

Does joining guarantee project work?

No. A project needs to fit your skills, availability and the business owner's requirements. We discuss the opportunity before anyone commits.

Do I need to use a particular AI tool?

Your approach should fit the business job, the existing tools and the handover needs. Explain the trade-offs of the tools you propose.

How do pricing and payment work?

Commercial terms are agreed for each project. That includes who contracts with whom, the project price, payment milestones and any XASIS fee.

Can I show a demo instead of client work?

Yes. Label it clearly and show what it does, how it was built and what its limits are. We still need to review whether it fits a real project.
