Straight answers

What we say no to

  • AI that quotes prices or gives advice without a person checking it.
  • AI phone receptionists.
  • Replacing your main software, or building an app from scratch.
  • Open-ended work with no fixed outcome.

Questions

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. Tell us how the work is done today. Working out the AI part is our job.

Who actually builds it?

An independent AI builder we have vetted. You deal with us, and we are responsible for the result.

What if it cannot be built?

We tell you within two working days. If it is not a good fit for AI, we say so.
